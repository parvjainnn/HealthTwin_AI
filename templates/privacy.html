Privacy

Privacy Policy

Last updated: September 20, 2026

HealthTwin is an educational health-support application. This policy explains the information we collect, how we use it, and the choices available to you.

Information you provide

When you create an account, we store your username, email address, and a securely hashed password. Information you enter into health tools, saved logs, chat prompts, and uploaded records is processed to provide the feature you request and is associated with your account.

How information is used

We use your information to operate the service, maintain account access, display your saved health workspace, process prediction requests, and improve reliability and security. HealthTwin is not a substitute for clinical care and does not use your information to provide a medical diagnosis.

Documents and health information

Uploaded records are stored so that you can access them from your account. Do not upload information you are not authorized to share. Please contact the site operator before using HealthTwin for regulated clinical workflows or where a specific health-data agreement is required.

Cookies, local storage, and analytics

Essential browser storage remembers your theme, language, and cookie choice. Optional privacy-friendly analytics is disabled until you accept it in the consent banner. If enabled by the site operator, it measures aggregate page views and interaction events; it is not loaded before consent. You may withdraw consent by clearing this site’s browser storage and choosing “Essential only” when prompted again.

Sharing and retention

We do not sell personal information. Information may be processed by service providers needed to host and operate HealthTwin. We retain account information and saved content while your account is active, subject to operational and legal requirements.

Security and your choices

We use reasonable safeguards, but no online system is completely secure. Use a unique password and keep your device secure. You may request access, correction, or deletion of your account information by contacting the site operator.

Contact and changes

For privacy questions or requests, contact the HealthTwin site operator at the contact channel provided with this service. We may update this policy as the product changes; the date above shows the latest revision.
