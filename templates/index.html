Questions, answered

Clear about what HealthTwin is for.

HealthTwin provides educational health support—not medical diagnosis or emergency care.

What can I do with HealthTwin?+

You can track daily health inputs, use supported prediction tools, save medical records, view history, and ask MediBot educational health questions.

Does HealthTwin replace a clinician?+

No. HealthTwin is designed to support understanding and preparation. Always seek advice from a qualified healthcare professional for diagnosis and treatment.

Are predictions medical diagnoses?+

No. Prediction results are educational model outputs based on the information provided. They are not a clinical diagnosis.

Where can I find my saved records?+

Your uploaded medical documents are available in the Records area of your HealthTwin workspace.

What information does MediBot provide?+

MediBot provides educational health information and explanations. It does not provide a medical diagnosis, prescribe treatment, or replace emergency services.
